About BOB’sLAB

BOB’sLAB is a VR physics sandbox developed by 26Th Studio. Players can experiment, build, and interact with a simulated environment driven by physics-based gameplay.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30/01/2026

1. Data We Collect

BOB’sLAB collects a User ID provided by the Meta Horizon platform. This identifier is used to associate in-game data with a user account and does not include personal information such as name, email, or payment details.

When in-app purchases are used, we receive limited purchase-related information from Meta Horizon to fulfill transactions and grant purchased content.

2. How We Use This Data

User ID is used for core application functionality, including saving progress, restoring content, and ensuring purchased items are correctly granted to the user.

Purchase-related data is used solely to complete in-app purchases and unlock optional downloadable content.

3. Payment Information

BOB’sLAB does not collect, store, or process payment information. All payments are handled directly by Meta Horizon in accordance with their policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties. No data is used for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

5. Data Retention

User ID and purchase-related data are retained only as long as necessary to provide application functionality and access to purchased content.
