About BOB’sLAB

BOB’sLAB is a creative VR physics sandbox designed and developed by 26Th Studio. Players can experiment, build, interact with objects, and explore a world driven by advanced physics simulation. This page provides all required legal, privacy, and support information for Meta Quest Store submission and public availability.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 09/12/2025

1. Data Collection (VRC.Privacy.1)

BOB’sLAB does not collect any personally identifiable information. No analytics systems, tracking technologies, advertising tools, or user-identifying services are used within the game.

2. Use of Data (VRC.Privacy.2)

Because no user data is collected, no information is used, processed, or stored for any purpose.

3. Third-Party Sharing (VRC.Privacy.3)

BOB’sLAB does not share, transmit, distribute, or sell user information to any third parties. No external parties ever receive player data.
